How OBLIVIA Works

A minor rights lifecycle protocol enabling guardians to enforce right-to-erasure under specific statutory frameworks with on-chain, court-ready cryptographic receipts.

Core Workflows

Statutory Lifecycles

Four interconnected workflows govern how minor identity is enrolled, encrypted, shredded, and vested.

  • 01

    Enrollment & Policy Pinning

    The guardian opens a protected subject bound to a live on-chain policy version (COPPA, MODPA, GDPR, DPDP). Only pseudonyms and hashes are recorded on chain. Zero personal data.

    Enroll
  • 02

    Client-Side Encrypted Storage

    Memories and logs are encrypted in the guardian's browser using WebCrypto AES-256-GCM. The hub only stores ciphertext blobs. The guardian commits the Merkle data-root on chain.

    Vault
  • 03

    Erasure & Dual-Notary Witness

    The guardian issues a wallet transaction proving legal authority. The operation is queued and executed via either Attestor EIP-191 signatures or Chainlink CRE forwarders. A DEC v2 certificate hash is permanently pinned on chain, and the hub shreds all corresponding ciphertext.

    Shred
  • 04

    Statutory Rights Vesting

    Upon reaching the statutory threshold age, guardian authority automatically expires. The principal (now an adult) claims their own sovereign digital rights.

    Vest
